# New Starter Onboarding Guide

Last edited by Sam Whitfield on 15 March 2025 · 5 min read

Welcome to Kitewing! This checklist walks you through your first weeks. Your manager and your onboarding buddy will help you with each step.

## Before your first day

- You will receive an email with your PeopleHub login. Check that your personal details and bank details are correct.
- Your laptop will be shipped to your home, or you can collect it from the Austin or Leeds office on your first day.

## Day one

- Sign in to your laptop and set up the Kitewing Authenticator app for multi-factor authentication. If you see error KSW-1043, call the IT Helpdesk on extension 4357.
- Meet your manager and your onboarding buddy. Your buddy is your go-to person for everyday questions during your first three months.
- Join your team's channels and add your photo to your PeopleHub profile.

## Your first two weeks

- Complete the security awareness training within 14 days of your start date.
- Read the Employee Handbook for your country.
- US employees: enroll in benefits in PeopleHub within 30 days of your start date.
- If you need extra equipment, such as a second monitor, request it with Form IT-207 in KiteDesk.

## Your first month

- Read and acknowledge the Code of Conduct within 30 days of joining.
- Agree your first objectives with your manager.

## Probation

Your manager will hold check-ins during your probation period. Probation lasts 90 days in the US and six months in the UK. The details are in the Employee Handbook for your country.

## Who to ask

- Everyday questions: your onboarding buddy
- HR and policies: people@kitewing.example (US) or people-uk@kitewing.example (UK)
- Laptops, accounts and access: it-help@kitewing.example or extension 4357
