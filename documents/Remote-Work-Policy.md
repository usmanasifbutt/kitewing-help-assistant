# Remote Work Policy

Last edited by Priya Raman on 1 February 2025 · 6 min read

On this page: Who this applies to | Work patterns | Core hours | Your home workspace | Working from another country | Security

## Who this applies to

This policy applies to all Kitewing employees in the US and the UK. Contractors should follow the terms of their contract.

## Work patterns

Hybrid is the default work pattern at Kitewing. Hybrid employees work from a Kitewing office at least two days a week, on their team's anchor days. For most teams the anchor days are Tuesday and Wednesday.

Some roles can be fully remote. Fully remote working must be approved by your department head and the People Team, and is recorded in PeopleHub.

## Core hours

Everyone is expected to be available during core hours, 10:00 to 16:00 in your local time zone, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours you can arrange your day with your manager. You are not expected to read or reply to messages outside your working hours.

## Your home workspace

Your home workspace must be safe, reasonably private, and suitable for confidential calls. Kitewing supports your home setup with a remote-work stipend and a one-time equipment allowance. The amounts, and how they are paid, are set out in the Travel and Expense Policy.

## Working from another country

You may work from a country other than your country of employment for up to 20 working days in any calendar year. You must get approval at least 3 weeks before you travel by submitting Form HR-118 (Work From Abroad Request) in PeopleHub. Some countries are excluded for security and tax reasons; the current list is on the form. Working abroad for more than 20 working days in a year needs approval from the Legal team as well.

## Travelling to an office

Hybrid employees' commuting costs between home and their usual office are not reimbursed. If you are fully remote and your manager asks you to travel to an office, the trip is business travel under the Travel and Expense Policy, so your travel and hotel costs are covered.

## Time zones and meetings

When your team works across the US and the UK, schedule recurring meetings between 15:00 and 17:00 UK time (09:00 to 11:00 Central Time) where possible, so that nobody has to join very early or very late. Record important meetings for colleagues who can't attend.

## Security when working remotely

Always connect through the Kitewing VPN when you work outside an office. Do not work on Confidential or Restricted information over public Wi-Fi unless the VPN is connected. Lock your screen whenever you step away, even at home.
